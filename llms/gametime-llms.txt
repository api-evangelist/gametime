# Gametime

> Gametime is a mobile-first ticket marketplace for last-minute tickets to sports, concerts, comedy, and theater across the United States and Canada. Buyers can purchase up to roughly 90 minutes after an event starts, with all-in (fee-inclusive) pricing, two-tap checkout, and mobile ticket delivery. Gametime does not publish a public developer API; it operates a B2B "API Seller" program for professional point-of-sale sellers.
